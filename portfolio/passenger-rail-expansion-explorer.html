Passenger Rail Expansion Explorer

One of my big passions is transportation. This project is the culmination of a year's work of web app building. ... This project is a two-for-one: The first map visualizes passenger rail expansion in Wisconsin. The various features here include both currently-operating and proposed passenger rail lines such as the Borealis, and the proposed Hiawatha West lines, respectively. Other features include economic impact zones (as denoted by the big circles), destinations, such as Green Bay Packers football, Wisconsin Badgers football & basketball, Summerfest, the Blue Ox fest, and more. Additionally, there's a network of bus routes for connecting people to cities without passenger rail service.
